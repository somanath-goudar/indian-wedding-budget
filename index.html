Frequently Asked Questions
The average Indian wedding cost in 2025 ranges from ₹5 lakhs for a small intimate ceremony to ₹1 crore+ for a large destination wedding. North Indian weddings average ₹15–50 lakhs, South Indian ₹8–25 lakhs, West Indian ₹10–35 lakhs, and East Indian ₹6–20 lakhs for 200–500 guests.
Catering is typically the largest cost at 30–40% of budget, followed by Venue (15–25%), Decoration (10–15%), and Photography & Video (8–12%). Jewellery can dominate the budget in South Indian weddings. Bridal wear and DJ/Band are significant in North Indian celebrations.
Indian wedding catering costs per head vary by region: North India ₹800–2,500/head, South India ₹500–1,500/head, West India ₹700–2,000/head, East India ₹400–1,200/head. A vegetarian menu costs less; multi-cuisine live counters cost significantly more.
Limit guest count (catering is your biggest variable cost), choose a weekday or off-peak date for 20–30% venue discount, combine ceremonies into fewer days, opt for a local venue over resort, hire a single multi-talented photographer, and use digital invitations instead of printed cards.
North Indian weddings (Punjab, Delhi, UP, Rajasthan) tend to cost more overall due to larger guest lists, multi-day ceremonies, and heavy decoration expectations. South Indian weddings are generally simpler in ceremony but can involve significant gold jewellery expenditure. A comparable 400-guest wedding might cost ₹30–60L in North India vs ₹15–30L in South India.
