Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Indian Wedding Budget Planner ("the Tool") is a free, client-side web application. We take your privacy seriously.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. All budget calculations happen entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.

Cookies

We do not use cookies or any local storage beyond what Chart.js requires to render charts.

Third-Party Services

We load Chart.js and jsPDF from public CDNs (jsDelivr, Cloudflare). These CDNs may log your IP address as part of standard request logging. We have no access to those logs.

Share Links

The share link encodes your budget data as a URL hash. This data is never transmitted to our servers — it lives only in the URL you share.
